Pentagon Boxed

Find the hidden word that uses every letter — or chain shorter words to cover them all.

How to play
  • 10 letters sit on the pentagon, two per side. Click letters to spell a word.
  • Consecutive letters cannot be on the same side.
  • Press Enter Word to lock it in. The next word starts with the previous word's last letter.
  • Use every letter at least once across all your words to win.
  • The intended answer is a single well-known word (10+ letters). Find it for a perfect solve.
  • Words are checked against a real English dictionary. Minimum 3 letters per word.
