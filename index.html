Pentagon Boxed

Letter Boxed, but five-sided. Solve in one word — or chain as many as you need.

How to play
  • Spell a word by clicking letters. Consecutive letters cannot be on the same side.
  • Press Enter Word to lock in a word. The next word must start with that word's last letter.
  • Use every letter on the pentagon at least once across all your words to win.
  • Bonus challenge: there's a single 15-letter English word that solves it in one shot.
  • Words are checked against a real English dictionary. Minimum 3 letters per word.
